Two posts: the integration guide (setup technote) and the dashboard cards. The guide links to the cards.

## Post 1: integration guide

- **Title:** Modern Flames Wi-Fi fireplaces in Home Assistant, fully local
- **Description:** Move a Modern Flames Landscape Pro Slim or PRO V2 Wi-Fi fireplace from the Modern Flames app to Smart Life, fetch its local key, and add it to Home Assistant with tuya-local, with no cloud afterwards. You get power, heat, three RGB colour zones, scenes, flame speed and the off timer, plus troubleshooting and example automations.
- **Tags:** home-assistant, tuya-local, tuya, modern-flames, fireplace, smart-home, local-control, technote

## Post 2: dashboard cards

- **Title:** Modern Flames cards for Home Assistant: minimal glow or live fire
- **Description:** Four dashboard cards for Modern Flames fireplaces that glow in the fireplace's real flame, ember and downlight colours: a hero card, chips, an overview tile and an all-in-one Studio card. Pick a calm drifting glow or a real-time WebGL fire, then restyle the buttons, corners, size and colours to match your dashboard.
